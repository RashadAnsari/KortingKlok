Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 February 2026

KortingKlok handles your data with care. Here's a brief overview of what we collect, why, and how.

1. What we collect

  • Account — name and email address (or Google/Apple account data when using social login).
  • App usage — tracked products, preference settings and push notification token.
  • Technical — anonymised usage statistics via Google Analytics (on the website only with your consent, in the app to improve user experience) and crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics).

2. Why

Solely to operate the app: account management, push notifications for tracked products, and technical improvements. We never sell your data to third parties and do not use it for advertising.

3. Third parties

We use Google Firebase (authentication, push notifications, crash reports) and Google Analytics (website and app statistics; on the website only after consent). Both are bound by data processing agreements and comply with GDPR.

4. Retention

  • Account data is deleted within 30 days of closing your account.
  • Analytical data: up to 14 months.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete and port your data. Send a request to hallo@kortingklok.nl.

6. Cookies

We use functional cookies (required, no consent needed) and analytical cookies via Google Analytics (only after your consent via the cookie banner).

7. Contact

Questions? Email hallo@kortingklok.nl.

Contact: hallo@kortingklok.nl
