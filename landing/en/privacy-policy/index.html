Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 February 2026

KortingKlok values your privacy. In this Privacy Policy we explain clearly and transparently which data we collect, why we collect it and how we handle it. This policy applies to all KortingKlok services, including our website and mobile app.

1. Who are we?

KortingKlok is a service that helps consumers in the Netherlands keep track of discounts and deals at supermarkets (Albert Heijn, Jumbo and Lidl). You can reach us at:

  • Email: hallo@kortingklok.nl
  • Website: kortingklok.nl

2. What data do we collect?

Depending on how you use our services, we may collect the following data:

2.1 Waitlist (website)

  • Email address — when you sign up for our waitlist.

2.2 Account (mobile app)

  • Name and email address — when registering with email and password.
  • Google or Apple account data — when registering via social login (name, email address and profile picture from the respective platform).

2.3 App usage

  • Tracked products — the products you have selected to follow.
  • Preference settings — language preference and theme settings.
  • Push notification token — a device identifier for sending notifications.

2.4 Technical data

  • Website usage — anonymised statistical data about visitor behaviour, collected via Google Analytics (only with your consent).
  • Crash reports — technical error reports via Firebase Crashlytics, so we can improve the app.

3. Why do we use your data?

We use your data exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Sending you an email when KortingKlok launches (waitlist).
  • Creating and managing your account in the app.
  • Sending push notifications when products you follow go on sale.
  • Improving our service based on anonymised usage statistics.
  • Resolving technical issues via crash reports.

We never sell your data to third parties. We do not use your data for profiling, advertising or any other commercial purpose outside our own service.

4. Legal basis for processing

We process your personal data on the following legal grounds as set out in the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) — for collecting analytical data via cookies and for sending marketing communications.
  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) — for providing our service, managing your account and sending product notifications.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — for the technical improvement of our service based on crash reports.

5. Third parties and sub-processors

To deliver our service, we use the following third-party providers. These parties only receive the data necessary for their services and are contractually required to protect your data:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database, push notifications and crash reports. Data may be stored within the European Economic Area (EEA). See the Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics — anonymised website statistics (only with your consent via the cookie banner). See the Google Privacy Policy.

6. How long do we keep your data?

  • Waitlist email addresses — up to 30 days after the launch of KortingKlok, or sooner if you unsubscribe.
  • Account data — for as long as you have an active account. After deleting your account, your data will be removed within 30 days.
  • Analytical data — up to 14 months, in line with Google Analytics default settings.
  • Crash reports — up to 90 days.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access — you can request what data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — you can request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten") — you can request deletion of your data.
  • Right to restriction of processing — you can request that processing be restricted in certain circumstances.
  • Right to data portability — you can request your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Right to object — you can object to the processing of your data on the basis of legitimate interests.
  • Right to withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

To exercise your rights, send an email to hallo@kortingklok.nl. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

If you believe we are not handling your data correctly, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) at autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl.

8. Cookies

Our website uses cookies. A cookie is a small text file placed on your device when you visit our website.

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Functional cookies — necessary for storing your cookie preference. These do not require consent.
  • Analytical cookies — Google Analytics to measure website traffic. These are only placed after your explicit consent via the cookie banner.

You can withdraw your consent for analytical cookies at any time by adjusting your browser settings or by contacting us.

9. Security

We take the protection of your data seriously and implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to prevent misuse, loss, unauthorised access or unwanted disclosure. All data traffic is secured via HTTPS encryption and authentication is handled through Firebase Authentication.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The most recent version will always be published on this page with the date of the latest update. For significant changes, we will notify you by email (if you are on our waitlist or have an account).

11. Contact

If you have any questions, comments or requests about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

  • Email: hallo@kortingklok.nl
